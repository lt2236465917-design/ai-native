# Play selection (Team example)

| Play | State | Reason | Next gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plan | ready | intent home and product owner are available | product owner accepts `intent.md` |
| Design | ready after Plan | UX and security standards have owners | product + engineering approve `spec.md` |
| Build | deferred | current sprint has no pilot slot | accepted `plan.md` and branch |
| Test | deferred | eval data is being prepared | runnable fixture and acceptance cases |

Selection follows prerequisites; the team is not adopting all six Plays at once.
